Scope: the whole DevCodex app shell and its five sections (Binder home, Learn + articles, Shorts, Trivia, Practice). Mode: Read for articles, Operate for practice and the shell; Experience for shorts and pack opening.
Audience: beginner-to-intermediate software engineers on phones (primary) and desktops. Job: understand one concept, prove it, collect it, revisit it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Concepts are collectible cards; finishing an article opens a pack and the trivia deck is your binder. Refuses the category default of a grey course list with progress rings and XP bars.

OWN-WORLD: Binder-felt near-black ground (#121016), panels #1c1823/#262030, ink #f5f2f9. Three saturated track colours carry whole fields: Web #ff7a3d, Code #a387ff, Data #1fcfa9, each with a dark ink partner; reward gold #ffc94a for selection and collection. Cards: 5:7, 7px track-colour frame, numbered #001/030, level pips, art window. Bricolage Grotesque display, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next reading. Rounded pills and 20-28px radii.

STORY: The learner sees what to read next and what it will add to the binder, reads a calm article with one hands-on figure, opens the pack, then reviews cards by swiping until they stick; practice proves it in code.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: header (fanned-card mark, wordmark, cards-collected chip), a full-width track-coloured quest panel with the next article title at display size, one dark pill CTA and the face-down pack fanned below; floating dock with Binder/Learn/Shorts/Trivia/Practice. Desktop: quest + tracks left, binder sleeves right.

FORM: Collectible trading cards and binder, candidate 5 of the grounded list (assigned by the roll). seed:e0889be7cba1a30a

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
